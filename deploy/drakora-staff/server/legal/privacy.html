Drakora uses personal information for the services you choose to use, community administration and security. This notice covers the public website, support and report tickets, partnership requests, staff applications, staff dashboard and connected network services operated by Drakora.

It explains processing; it is not a request for blanket consent. Visiting the website does not authorize every possible use of your information. Where a feature needs optional consent, that choice must be asked for separately.

  • Provide only information relevant to your request. Do not send passwords, payment card details, private identification documents or unrelated sensitive information.
  • Access to private tickets, applications, inboxes and staff records depends on staff roles and permissions.
  • Information may be processed in the United States, Europe and by the providers described below.
  • You can make privacy requests without creating a staff account.

How information is stored and protected

Drakora uses a mix of privately operated machines in North Dakota, United States, and EU hosting from Kinetic Hosting. Its EU proxy uses PebbleHost infrastructure. Databases may run on the private machines or Kinetic Hosting infrastructure, depending on the service. Midco provides connectivity for the private machines; it is not being described as Drakora’s application database host.

  • The website/staff portal, application and ticket record stores use separate SQLite databases with authenticated encryption of record payloads. Their encryption keys stay in protected deployment configuration.
  • HTTPS protects browser connections. Essential session and authorization checks protect private pages and mutations.
  • Ticket categories, application access, inbox access and moderation history use permission checks. Staff should see only records necessary for their authorized work.
  • Some metadata, database indexes, logs and external copies are not protected by the same record encryption. This policy does not claim that every server log, email, image, game world or provider backup is encrypted in the same way.
  • Messages and attachments sent through Discord, email or the store also exist in those systems. Deleting a portal record alone does not delete every external copy.
  • For guest ticket limits, the ticket service stores a keyed HMAC of the trusted connection IP rather than the raw IP in the ticket record. That marker is still treated as personal data. Hosting, network and security systems may separately process raw connection IPs.

No security measure can promise absolute protection. A security incident requires investigation and, when legally required, notification to affected people and the appropriate authority. We must record the assessment and follow applicable notification deadlines.

Retention and deletion

The portal automatically checks for inactivity every day. Ticket records, partnership requests and staff applications become eligible for deletion after 12 calendar months without activity. This applies to open and closed records. Activity means the owner submits, updates, replies, reopens or rates that record. Successful staff sign-in and intentional authenticated dashboard use renew linked account activity. Staff viewing, scheduled delivery, role synchronization and background polling do not renew someone else’s timer.

Verified Discord account activity can renew that account’s linked records. Unverified usernames or contact addresses do not renew another person’s records. Existing staff profiles without a trustworthy activity timestamp start their timer when this rule is enabled. Records with known submission activity keep their original timestamp. Data for different people is not reset together.

Expired portal records are deleted with their local conversation/history data, summaries, references and delivery queues. Bot-managed ticket channels, attachment messages and saved application notices are removed through Discord where the bot has access. A bot application update channel is removed when no application for its owner remains. External failures are recorded without message content and retried; a pending record is withheld from ordinary use while cleanup completes. Recipient downloads, email copies and independent provider records cannot be recalled by a database sweep.

Inactive portal staff profiles lose their stored OAuth credentials, Minecraft name link, interface preferences, activity summary and portal sessions. Huly workspace content, configured mailbox messages, Minecraft logs, game player files, Tebex accounting records and manual backups are separate systems. The portal’s automatic sweep does not erase those systems or use unconnected Minecraft gameplay as a renewal signal. Contact support for a coordinated request across them.

Ticket attachments have a shorter 30 day period, with unused uploads removed sooner. Application drafts expire after 7 days. Honeypot event details expire after 90 days; its minimal keyed repeat marker expires after 365 days. Staff activity summaries expire after 90 days. Role-change audit logs and mailbox reply markers expire after 12 months from the event. Essential login/session records and temporary caches expire earlier under their service rules. Shorter periods are not extended by the 12 month rule.

A specific legal obligation or dispute can require limited, restricted preservation. A hold needs a recorded reason, scope and review date; it is not permission to keep all information indefinitely. Privacy requests can seek earlier deletion or restriction subject to applicable rights and justified exceptions.

Manual backups currently require manual expiry and deletion. Their maximum retention has not yet been established, so deletion from a live database is not a promise of immediate removal from every backup. Before restoring an older copy, the operator must reapply inactivity deletion and confirmed privacy-request removals before opening it to users. Shared server worlds are retained for gameplay continuity, but embedded player identifiers and personal content still need assessment when a request concerns them.

Cookies, browser storage and external links

The public website uses local fire and smoke animation. Mouse movement affects the smoke within your browser only; those pointer coordinates are not sent to Drakora, stored or used for analytics. Artwork and animation code are served by Drakora without contacting the artwork provider. You can pause the animation, and reduced-motion preferences disable it. Mobile layouts show static scenery without these effects. The dragon introduction does not create a tracking cookie or a stored visitor identifier.

The portal uses essential session cookies for sign-in, access to private tickets or applications, and security. It also uses browser storage for interface preferences, application steps and temporary partnership form state. Essential security/session data is not an advertising consent choice.

You can remove Drakora site data in your browser. That can sign you out, reset preferences and remove local draft references; it does not delete submitted records from the server. Signing out does not erase a ticket, application or moderation record.

The portal does not add advertising or analytics scripts. If optional analytics, marketing cookies or similar tracking are added, their purposes, providers and choices need to be disclosed and consent requested before use where required.

Discord, the store, pack download sites and other linked services have their own notices. Opening a third-party link leaves the Drakora service. External images can still create provider requests while you stay on a Drakora page.

Your choices and privacy rights

Where the relevant law applies, you may ask to access or correct personal information, request deletion or restriction, receive a portable copy where that right applies, object to processing based on legitimate interests, withdraw optional consent, or request human review of a qualifying automated decision.

These rights have conditions and exceptions. A request to stop optional processing does not require us to stop every essential security function or erase records we must lawfully preserve. We should explain the specific reason for any refusal or remaining retention, rather than require you to waive rights to use the service.

  2. Tell us which service and right your request concerns. Include the relevant account name or ticket/application reference if available.

Where GDPR applies, the normal response deadline is one month. A complex request may need up to two additional months, with the extension and reason explained within the first month. Requests are ordinarily free, subject only to the limited exceptions allowed by law.

If a request is refused or only partly fulfilled, we must explain the reasons and available complaint options. You may complain to a competent data protection authority and pursue applicable remedies. You do not have to contact Drakora before exercising a legal complaint right.

Moderation, linked records and human review

The Drakora honeypot automatically applies a 24 hour Discord timeout after a first qualifying post and a permanent ban on a repeat event detected by its retained marker. It is intended to catch spam. The event records the account, action, timestamp and reason for authorized staff review.

Contact support to request review of a mistaken action. Staff must consider relevant context and correct errors. Whether a particular action falls within GDPR’s rules for solely automated decisions depends on its actual effect; this notice does not ask you to waive those protections.

Moderation history can appear beside tickets and staff applications when linked to a verified Discord ID. An unverified claimed username does not inherit someone else’s record. Application reviewers make the role decision; the portal’s shared history is context for a reviewer, not a hidden promise of an automatic rejection score.

Children, accounts and staff applicants

Staff applicants must be at least 18. Ordinary players must meet the applicable Minecraft/Microsoft account requirements and local law. Discord-connected features also require eligibility under Discord’s age rules, including its minimum age of 13 and any higher local requirement. These are separate requirements; a Minecraft or Discord account is not proof that someone is eligible to become staff.

You must give truthful age and eligibility information. Staff application age declarations are an eligibility check, not a guarantee of identity verification. Drakora does not routinely collect identity documents from every applicant. We may seek proportionate clarification when necessary, but you should not send a passport, identity card or other sensitive document through an ordinary ticket or Discord message.

Where the law permits, we may rely on information supplied by the user. An inaccurate declaration does not, by itself, prove that Drakora knew the person’s true age. However, credible information received by staff, including a parent’s report, cannot be ignored. We will review it, restrict an ineligible feature, stop collection that lacks a lawful basis and promptly remove improperly collected information where required. An under-18 staff application must not proceed as an adult application.

Where GDPR requires parental authorization for a consent-based online service offered directly to a child, the applicable national threshold can be between 13 and 16, and reasonable verification is required. A checkbox or another platform’s account does not supply that authorization for Drakora’s separate processing.

A parent, guardian or user can report an age or child-privacy concern to support@drakora.org with the subject Privacy request. Include only the relevant account or submission reference and the concern. We will request only information reasonably needed to assess it and explain the action taken, subject to protecting other people’s information.

Deletion of Drakora-controlled records is separate from deletion of an independently held Discord, Microsoft or checkout account. We will explain any provider action needed. A false age declaration does not waive statutory protections or excuse Drakora from a duty that applies to its actual service and audience.
