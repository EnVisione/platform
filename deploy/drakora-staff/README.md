# Drakora Staff

A React staff portal and Discord identity provider for Huly 0.7.426. The Dashboard role is required to sign in. Every active staff member receives a Huly account and workspace membership at dashboard sign-in. Tracker and Calendar use the same Dashboard admission role; their dashboard permission switches can further restrict access. Staff ranks determine Huly workspace permissions and appear as project roles.

The Discord service supplies staff ranks, activity, role synchronization and notifications. Office and its Discord controls are no longer exposed in the dashboard; existing Discord channels and stored records are preserved.

An optional Discord honeypot applies a 24 hour timeout for the first post in a clearly marked spam trap and a permanent ban for a repeat within 365 days. Its pinned warning shows a caught counter. Private moderation alerts and Manager or Founder commands provide status, logs, pause, resume and reviewed marker resets. The Moderation dashboard records the member's Discord name, username, action and reason, with the same history on verified staff application and ticket profiles. Access is controlled through Roles. See [honeypot configuration and moderation history](documentation.md#discord-honeypot).

The dashboard places the Drakora logo beside the staff name in its sidebar heading, with the same branding above navigation on mobile. There is no separate Discord server rail.

With `tickets` configured, players open private support tickets at `/help/new` or with `/ticket` in the community Discord. Both entry points offer dedicated intake forms for support, billing, bugs, exploits and reports. Player and staff reports ask for the reported person, incident location, timing and evidence; reproduction questions belong only to bug reports. Staff queues separate Support, Reports, Billing, Partnerships and Staff reports, with category permissions in Roles. All Dashboard staff can view Support and Reports by default. Billing is limited to Founders; Partnerships and staff reports are limited to Managers and Founders. Replying, claiming and resolving remain separate permissions. The website verifies Discord identity without creating another account. Staff use Tickets to claim and reply, then record the work, commands and optional proof when resolving the ticket. Resolutions accept any nonblank text. The shared macro library lets authorized staff save category-specific reply templates and insert them into a draft for review before sending. In customer and staff conversations, Enter sends a reply and Shift+Enter adds a new line. Tickets show Claimed by the assigned staff member, or Staff replied with the first replier’s name until somebody claims them. Claim and takeover announcements notify the Discord owner and assigned staff in their private channel; website guests receive email updates with the staff name. Admins, Managers and Founders can take over another staff member’s ticket when their role permits it for that category; the assignment change is recorded in history. Live web updates and a persistent Discord delivery queue keep both conversations connected. Managers and Founders alone can review staff reports.

Closed tickets appear in Logs, with text and history subject to the portal’s twelve month inactivity rule, separate staff and player HTML transcripts, an optional player rating from one to five, and attachment removal after thirty days. Closing a ticket removes the owner’s Discord access and retains the channel. Admins and higher can separately delete the closed channel once its transcript and retained attachments are safely saved. Owners and authorized staff can reopen tickets without losing earlier history or ratings. Channels confirmed missing in Discord are replaced for active or reopened tickets, and saved replies resume delivery. Discord posts separate closure, resolution, private feedback receipt and reopening notices. Closed-ticket notices include an Admin+ deletion button; deleting the channel keeps the saved history. Staff commands and server logs are labeled Not connected until their integrations exist. Minecraft ticket delivery and player statistics are disabled. See [support ticket configuration](documentation.md#support-tickets).

Staff lists support combined filters. Tickets and Logs filter by category, ticket type, status, assignment, person or issue text, and opening dates, with recent activity or sorting by opening time. Ticket event history, moderation records, application submissions and role activity also have search and date controls. Matching totals and pages follow the filters and current permissions. See [staff list filters](documentation.md#staff-list-filters).

Overview greets the signed-in staff member and shows live Minecraft endpoint status, player totals and pending work. Add each unique game server and proxy to `overview.servers` in private configuration; proxy counts are displayed separately and excluded from the total to avoid counting players twice. Visible pages refresh every fifteen seconds, with shared server probes across viewers. Unreachable servers and incomplete totals are labeled clearly. Staff application and email counts follow the existing review and mailbox permissions. Ticket categories and appeal forums can be connected through `overview.queues`; counts include only open items the viewer can access. Unconnected or unavailable queues never appear as zero. See [Overview configuration](documentation.md#overview) for queue mapping and email semantics.

The optional Email tab opens a shared Drakora inbox inside the dashboard. By default, Admins, Managers and Founders with Dashboard access can read and send mail. Managers and Founders can change email permissions separately in Roles. It supports folders, address and text filters, unread messages, replies, forwarding, stars and attachments, using the existing Discord sign-in. Replies open beneath the message with collapsed quoted history. New messages use a compact window with Cc and Bcc controls, sender selection, minimize, full screen and explicit discard. Closing a nonempty composer keeps the draft in the open page; reloading or leaving the page still warns before losing unsent content. Email bodies stay in Proton; the portal does not mirror them into its database. See [shared email setup](#shared-email-inbox).

Last active combines observed presence with message timestamps, including messages sent while invisible. Set `activityGuildIds` to the community guild ID to include its messages; the staff guild is always included. Only current staff guild members contribute activity records. The bot must be present and able to view the relevant channels. Read Message History permits a bounded refresh of the latest 100 messages in each readable channel and active thread after connection. Older and inaccessible history may be missing. Only the latest activity timestamp is retained, never message contents.

Huly Contacts shows the configured Discord staff ranks for linked employees. The rank badges refresh while Contacts is open. The generic Huly Worker badge is hidden in this deployment; its underlying HR data remains intact.

## Requirements

- A working [Huly self-hosted installation](https://github.com/hcengineering/huly-selfhost), matching this checkout's Huly version.
- Docker Compose and a shared private Docker network.
- Two HTTPS origins routed through this service, preserving the original Host header and setting X-Forwarded-Proto to https.
- A Discord application with OAuth2 and a bot in the staff guild and any community guild configured for activity. Enable Server Members Intent and Presence Intent. Message Content Intent is unnecessary.
- Node.js 24 and pnpm 11.19.0 for local portal development.

## Install

1. Copy `.env.example` to a private `.env`. Set persistent data and configuration paths outside the checkout and the actual Huly Docker network name.
2. Copy `config.example.json` to that configuration path. Replace every placeholder. Generate independent random session and OIDC secrets. Generate the database key using `openssl rand -base64 32`. Use Huly's existing shared token secret, workspace UUID, and owner account UUID. Keep this file readable only by the service user.
3. Configure Discord's OAuth redirect as `https://staff.example.com/auth/discord/callback`. The requested scopes are `identify email guilds.members.read`. Only a verified Discord email may establish an identity.
4. Create the Office category and channels described in [the technical documentation](documentation.md). Add the IDs to the private configuration. Keep rank entries in highest to lowest priority order.
5. Create the data directory for container user 1000. Run `docker compose build staff` and `docker compose up -d staff` from this directory.
6. Add the supplied `huly-auth.compose.yml` to the Huly Compose files. Set its variables privately. The OIDC secret must match `oidcClientSecret`, and `STAFF_SOURCE_PATH` points to this directory on the host. The issuer wait script resolves the first startup dependency.
7. Build the Huly frontend and account images below, set `HULY_OFFICE_IMAGE` and `HULY_ACCOUNT_IMAGE`, and recreate Huly's front and account services.
8. Route both public origins to this service on port 3000 through the private network, or to its loopback port 8088. Keep Huly's nginx and account services inaccessible directly from the public internet.

The staff root redirects unauthenticated users to `/login`. Discord sign-in is denied without the Dashboard role. Tracker and Calendar appear in the dashboard when the signed-in member has the corresponding saved permissions. Old `/huly`, `/todo` and `/office` links lead to Tracker. Calendar opens the existing Planner with its schedule and action items. Its All view lists every open issue in accessible active Tracker projects, including unassigned tasks and Discord imports, once per issue. Completed and cancelled issues and completed personal action items stay out of the Calendar task lists. Personal scheduling remains available through Unplanned and Planned, and through Schedule on tasks with an active personal planning copy. Due date is always available in the task properties panel. Tracker, Calendar task rows and project task scheduling editors use the same issue due date and native calendar picker. New event buttons under Planner and beside the schedule date controls open the full native event editor for the selected day. Tracker retains Issues, Components, Milestones, Templates, project settings and the existing GitHub and Discord integrations. Embedded views hide the duplicate app rail, global header and Office controls. Their task rows, status groups, calendar grid and buttons use the dashboard palette and saved accent. Tracker and Calendar preload after dashboard admission and remain loaded while switching dashboard tabs. Embedded access uses the dashboard session without a separate workspace sign-in. A connection failure offers recovery inside the dashboard; an expired dashboard session requires signing in again. Unsent email drafts remain open when switching dashboard tabs.

Settings offers 12 or 24 hour time, saved to the signed-in account. The staff header always uses the browser’s local timezone. Opening the dashboard records that timezone so Accounts can show the member’s current local time at the bottom right of their card, including daylight saving changes. Members who have not opened the dashboard since this feature was added show Local time unavailable. The displayed format follows the viewer’s preference, including the embedded Calendar hour labels and current-time marker.

After Discord sign-in, new and existing staff must submit a Minecraft Java Edition username before using the dashboard or Huly. Names are 3–16 letters, numbers, or underscores and cannot be claimed by another staff account, regardless of case. A submitted name is marked awaiting in-game verification, but staff may continue immediately. Verification and Minecraft server last-login activity require the later server integration. Staff may request a correction in Settings; by default, only a Founder or Manager can approve a name change. By default, Founder, Manager, and Admin can view registered accounts, current configured Discord staff ranks, and observed Discord activity on the Accounts page. Ranks are unavailable while the Discord Office connection is down. The activity record starts when the staff bot first sees a member online, retains observations for ninety days, and cannot reveal invisible presence or past history.

The configured community staff ranks, from highest to lowest, are Founder, Manager, Admin, Sr Moderator, Moderator, Jr Moderator, and Helper. Trial Staff is no longer configured. Manager uses the same Huly Maintainer permission level as Admin; the other new moderation ranks use User. The Dashboard role remains required for admission; the legacy Todo role no longer controls workspace access.

The optional `roleSync` configuration mirrors mapped ranks between Drakora Staff and the main Drakora server. Promotions, demotions, and rank removals work in either direction. On the first sync, staff-server ranks resolve conflicting assignments; a member ranked in only one server keeps that rank. If the member has not joined the other server, the assignment waits in encrypted storage and applies when they join and finish membership screening. The bot needs Manage Roles and must be above the ranks it copies in both servers. An inaccessible rank remains pending without blocking other members and retries after permissions change. Unmapped roles, including Dashboard and Todo access, are preserved. See [rank synchronization](documentation.md#discord-staff-rank-synchronization) for configuration and recovery behavior.

Signed-in staff can open Dashboard Settings to choose a 3 or 6 digit hex accent color. Save applies it across the dashboard, email compose and replies, application controls, Roles and the embedded Tracker and Calendar. It keeps the color across refreshes for that Discord account in the same browser. Muted accents and readable text follow the selected color, while status and warning colors retain their meaning. Reset restores the default Discord blue. This appearance setting stays in browser storage and does not sync across devices.

The Drakora Huly frontend hides the Chat and HR applications from workspace navigation. Staff communication stays in Discord. Existing Huly chat channels and meeting minutes remain stored; hiding the applications does not delete their records.

The optional `todoForums` configuration links Discord forum to-do lists to Huly Tracker projects. Tasks, titles, assignments, workflow statuses, and comments synchronize in both directions. New Tracker replies use webhooks with the author's name and profile picture, without a message embed. Grant the bot Manage Webhooks on the mapped forums. Existing bot replies retain their links for edits and deletions. Missing assignee tags are created for linked staff members while the forum has room. Tracker's Void label and selected priority appear as Discord title prefixes, for example `[VOID] [HIGH] Task name`, without changing the Tracker title or consuming forum tags. No priority leaves out the priority prefix. Deleting a linked task or comment removes its counterpart. Archived posts remain linked and retain their archived state after updates. Tracker and Discord events prompt a sync after 750 milliseconds, with a 30 second recovery poll and Discord rate limit handling. Each issue has a `Discord post` link. Create that URL property on Huly's Issue type and the workflow statuses listed in [the technical documentation](documentation.md) before enabling the mapping. Staff assignees need a linked Discord identity. The technical documentation covers assignee tags, permissions, conflict handling, and recovery.

## Staff applications

The connected Discord account on the introduction screen includes a **Not you? Sign out** button. It disconnects that account from the application while preserving the draft. Reconnecting opens Discord authorization rather than automatically reusing a signed-in dashboard account. Dashboard and Huly sessions are unaffected.

Required questions and fields have a red asterisk. Portfolio and evidence questions link to Imgur for screenshot hosting. Applicants paste the image, album, portfolio, or other public reference links into their answers. Evidence or references remains optional and also accepts up to five public HTTP or HTTPS links in a separate field. Image uploads are unavailable, and the service does not download or store linked screenshots. Authorized reviewers can open evidence links from the private submission.

The optional `applications` configuration serves a public form at the separate `publicOrigin` under `/apply/start`. Without `website` configuration, `/apply` also opens the form. Route that hostname to this service while preserving Host and HTTPS forwarding. Without `website` configuration, its root redirects to `/apply`. With `website` configured, the root serves the public Drakora website. Staff APIs and Huly are unavailable on the public hostname. Existing staff and Huly hostnames retain their authentication requirements.

Applicants first enter a preferred name and can optionally connect Discord before choosing Community Staff, Builder, Artist, or Developer. Discord can be connected before entering a name, but a preferred name is always required to continue. Registered staff who connect Discord confirm the Minecraft name already linked in their panel. If it is incorrect, they must request a correction in panel Settings and wait for Founder or Manager approval. A pending correction blocks application submission, and an approved name change requires fresh confirmation. Other applicants enter a Minecraft Java Edition username. Applicants then answer questions for their role and selected Drakora communities. Discord sign-in uses the existing staff OAuth callback. It confirms only identity and does not create a staff account or require membership in the staff guild. Applicants without a verified Discord email must provide a contact email. Discord bios are not collected. Current community staff can apply for specialist roles they do not hold. Specialist staff can apply for Community Staff and other specialist roles they do not hold. Applicants with neither see all four options.

Applicants must confirm that they are 18 or older, acknowledge the Privacy Policy and accept the Terms of Service. Community Staff receives one randomly selected question from twenty scenarios; specialist roles receive a scenario relevant to their work. The assigned scenario stays with the draft. Drafts expire after seven days and remain associated with that browser's secure session. Submitted applications follow the twelve month inactivity rule described below. Minecraft lookup and head previews confirm a public profile only, never account ownership. Server history and in-game verification are not connected yet.

Set `STAFF_APPLICATIONS_DATA_PATH` to a private persistent directory owned by container user 1000. The application SQLite database uses an independent random `applications.databaseKey`. Keep both the database and key backed up privately. Do not commit application data or private configuration. See [the technical documentation](documentation.md) for delivery and access boundaries.

Applicants who do not use Discord receive a final required Yes/No question about downloading it for staff communication if approved. Either answer allows submission and is shown to staff. Applicants who already use or connect Discord skip this question.

Application notices go to `applications.notificationChannelId` with all mentions disabled. They contain the preferred name, role, optional Discord mention, and a link to the private application. Configure the channel as read-only for members and allow the bot to view, send messages, embed links, and read history. Jr Moderator, Moderator, Sr Moderator, Admin, Manager, and Founder members with the Dashboard role can read the Staff Applications list and individual submissions and leave private feedback. Each application supports up to 100 feedback entries of 4,000 characters. Feedback shows the author's Discord avatar beside their name, using cached staff profiles for existing comments and initials when no picture is available. Images remain on Discord's CDN. Applications do not synchronize to Huly.

By default, only Managers and Founders can start reviewing, approve, or deny applications. Those actions have separate permission switches in Roles. Starting review records the reviewer and time and changes the list status to Reviewing. Decisions are final, record the reviewer and time, and do not automatically grant Discord roles. A denial uses a generic message when no custom message is provided and accepts a waiting period from 0 to 365 whole days, with seven days selected by default. Zero adds no waiting period. That wait applies only to the denied application type and is enforced when submitting, including from a new draft. Known waiting periods appear beside application types. Feedback, review, and decisions recheck Discord roles when saved.

Applicants who link Discord choose Discord updates or opt out and provide a contact email. DMs confirm submission and announce review, approval, or denial with teal, blue, green, and red accents, personal greetings, the applicant's avatar when available, and clear next steps. A denial includes the complete staff message and earliest reapplication date. With `applications.fallback` configured, blocked DMs use one private text channel per applicant in the main Drakora server. The applicant must be a member of that server. Only the applicant, configured reviewer roles, the bot, and Discord administrators can access it. Channels allow replies and remain open until the operator removes them. Failed delivery never loses an application.

Email choices require an explicit address, including for applicants whose Discord account has a verified email. With `applications.smtp` configured, submission confirmations and review, approval, and denial updates are sent through Proton Bridge. Emails include a personal greeting, colored status, staff decision message, and reapplication date when denied. Email choices do not send applicant DMs or create fallback channels. Without SMTP configuration, the encrypted email outbox remains available for later delivery and staff can follow up manually. The staff view and receipt distinguish queued mail, acceptance by the email service, and failed delivery. Acceptance does not guarantee arrival in the recipient's inbox. The receipt is bound to the submitting browser session for seven days and lets applicants change future notification preferences. Already delivered messages remain in place.

### Proton Bridge email delivery

Run a signed-in Proton Bridge on the same host as the staff container. Use its generated SMTP password, rather than the Proton account password. Install `proton-bridge-smtp.socket` and `proton-bridge-smtp.service` in the host user's systemd unit directory and enable the socket with `systemctl --user enable --now proton-bridge-smtp.socket`. The supplied service expects `protonmail-bridge.service`, `/usr/lib/systemd/systemd-socket-proxyd`, and Bridge's loopback SMTP port 1025. Adjust those deployment paths when needed. The socket directory is private to that user; its numeric UID must match the container user 1000. Keep user services running after logout with the host's normal linger configuration.

Set `STAFF_MAIL_SOCKET_PATH` to that user's `.local/share/drakora-mail` directory. The container mounts it at `/run/mail`. In private configuration, set `applications.smtp` with `socketPath` set to `/run/mail/smtp.sock`, Bridge `user` and `password`, the enabled sender `from`, optional `replyTo`, and `ca` containing Bridge's PEM certificate. STARTTLS and certificate verification are required. The supplied Bridge connection verifies the certificate for 127.0.0.1. Never expose Bridge's ports publicly or disable certificate verification. Update the trusted certificate if Bridge regenerates it. Sender addresses must already exist in the signed-in Proton account. See [Proton's Bridge CLI guide](https://proton.me/support/bridge-cli-guide) for connection settings.

The notification channel also receives blue review, green acceptance, and red denial updates identifying the acting Manager or Founder and applicant. Denials include the reason and the date and time remaining before that application type can be submitted again. Each update links to the application and reports the applicant's delivery route, failure, or queued update at posting time. Mentions stay silent, private feedback stays in the panel, and existing statuses are not backfilled.

The receipt also lists your current and previous applications with submission dates and colored review statuses. Refresh updates reloads the list. History matches the connected Discord account or applications submitted without Discord in the same browser session. Contact email and Minecraft name alone cannot retrieve another applicant's history. The list uses pages of twenty applications and does not include private staff feedback.

The staff review side panel lists the applicant's other stored applications, with application type, Minecraft name, submission date, colored current status, and a link to each review. It excludes the application being viewed and uses pages of five. Linked submissions match the same Discord account. If either submission has no Discord account, both the contact email and Minecraft name must match. These anonymous matches are claimed identities, not proof of ownership. This staff history remains private to authorized reviewers and does not expand public receipt access.

## Shared email inbox

After configuring Proton Bridge email delivery, install `proton-bridge-imap.socket` and `proton-bridge-imap.service` beside the SMTP units. Enable the socket with `systemctl --user enable --now proton-bridge-imap.socket`. It relays the private Unix socket to Bridge's loopback IMAP port 1143, sharing the same UID, directory and container mount as SMTP. No public mail listener is needed.

Add the optional `mail` block to private configuration:

```json
{
  "mail": {
    "imapSocketPath": "/run/mail/imap.sock",
    "identities": [
      { "address": "support@drakora.org", "name": "Drakora Support" },
      { "address": "no-reply@drakora.org", "name": "Drakora" }
    ]
  }
}
```

Use only addresses already enabled in the connected Proton account. The inbox reuses `applications.smtp` credentials and its pinned Bridge certificate. Add each address staff should receive and send from to `identities`; unknown sender addresses are rejected. Views search the configured addresses in sender, recipient and delivery headers. Standard mail folders and labels named for the configured address domains are available. To expose other shared folders, add their exact IMAP paths to optional `mail.folderPaths`. Personal folder labels remain hidden. All mail includes matching messages filed elsewhere. Incoming mail with no configured address in those headers is outside this view. Remove `mail` to disable the tab.

The mailbox layout uses the dashboard accent and adapts to mobile screens. Opening an email shows its subject and sender immediately while the message loads, then marks it read for staff with read-status permission. Mark unread remains available. The message list has a delete control for staff with delete permission; it moves emails to Trash and refreshes the list. Admin, Manager and Founder receive delete permission by default, and Roles can change it separately. The portal does not permanently delete mail. The selected message is checked against the configured addresses directly, without repeating a search of the mailbox. Replies retain the original email thread. HTML messages are sanitized, scripts and remote images are removed, and attachment downloads require the same staff access checks. Staff can attach up to five files totaling 10 MB; files remain in memory until sent. Drafts stay only in the open page, with a warning before discarding or navigating away. Sending stores a small encrypted receipt for thirty days to prevent duplicate submission of the same draft. If the result is uncertain, check Sent before composing another message. Proton acceptance does not guarantee arrival in the recipient's inbox.

The email client uses [ImapFlow](https://github.com/postalsys/imapflow), [MailParser](https://github.com/nodemailer/mailparser), Nodemailer and sanitize-html with a portal interface. Existing application notification delivery remains independent of interactive mail.

To enable staff Discord alerts, add `mail.notifications` with `categoryId` set to a category in the staff server. The bot creates a private `email` text channel and checks the shared INBOX every minute. Alerts use a blue accent, identify the shared recipient, and link to the dashboard mailbox. Sender details, subjects, bodies and attachments remain in the dashboard. The initial connection establishes a baseline without posting old mail. Checkpoints survive restarts; failed sends retry with a stable Discord nonce and check recent alerts before resending an uncertain delivery. A mailbox UID validity reset establishes a new baseline.

Channel access follows each member's effective dashboard `mail.view` permission, including the Dashboard access role. Role and policy changes refresh access, with a periodic reconciliation each minute. Members can read alerts; the bot posts them. Discord administrators retain their platform override. The bot needs Manage Channels, View Channel, Read Message History, Send Messages and Embed Links. Up to 98 staff viewers fit Discord's channel overwrite limit; exceeding that limit closes member access and pauses alerts. Progress records contain message UIDs and delivery identifiers, not email content. Removing the notification configuration stops alerts; an existing channel remains for the operator to remove.

## Huly frontend image

From the platform repository root, using Node.js 20 through 24:

```sh
node common/scripts/install-run-rush.js install --to @hcengineering/prod
NODE_PATH="$PWD/dev/prod/node_modules" node common/scripts/install-run-rush.js package --to @hcengineering/prod --parallelism 6
docker build -f deploy/drakora-staff/Dockerfile.huly-front \
  --build-arg HULY_FRONT_IMAGE=your-existing-pinned-huly-front-image \
  -t drakora-huly-front:office .
```

Use the exact front image digest from the matching installation as the base. This image replaces browser assets while preserving the matching front server. Never mix platform tags and server versions. The optional `OFFICE_URL` browser configuration activates Discord controls in Huly's original Office layout; deployments without it retain Huly's original Office. Room tiles still open Huly's room view. Huly audio and video calls require a separately configured Love service and LiveKit endpoint; Discord voice links remain independent.

## Huly account image

Build the account overlay from the exact pinned account image digest used by the matching installation:

```sh
docker build -f deploy/drakora-staff/Dockerfile.huly-account \
  --build-arg HULY_ACCOUNT_IMAGE=your-existing-pinned-huly-account-image \
  -t drakora-huly-account:staff-access deploy/drakora-staff
```

Set `HULY_ACCOUNT_IMAGE=drakora-huly-account:staff-access` in the private Huly environment. The overlay adds a trusted staff provisioning method used at dashboard sign-in and preserves account creation through valid, email-bound OpenID workspace invitations. Public signup remains disabled. Provisioning keeps the stable Discord identity, links an existing verified-email account, and rejects conflicting identities. Build from the original pinned upstream account image, not an already patched overlay. The build fails if either expected account handler changes. Rebuild and review the overlay when changing the base image digest.

## Self-host deployment templates

`huly-production.compose.yml`, `huly-images.compose.yml`, and `huly.nginx.conf` preserve the deployment customizations as templates. They target self-host commit `ca3808a57c83518b971343df7d073753f38796ed` with Huly 0.7.426. Copy the nginx template privately and replace its example hostname.

Set `HULY_NGINX_CONFIG`, `TODO_ORIGIN`, and `HULY_TUNNEL_TOKEN_PATH` in the private Huly environment, along with the base self-host configuration. Generate the MinIO and database credentials privately. Set `HTTP_BIND=127.0.0.1` and keep the persistent volume paths outside the checkout. Use the production override before the image pins, and the auth override last so the custom front image takes precedence:

```sh
docker compose --env-file /private/huly.conf \
  -f compose.yml \
  -f /source/deploy/drakora-staff/huly-production.compose.yml \
  -f /source/deploy/drakora-staff/huly-images.compose.yml \
  -f /source/deploy/drakora-staff/huly-auth.compose.yml up -d
```

The tunnel override assumes a private Cloudflare Tunnel token file. Configure both public hostnames to target the staff service. If using a different TLS proxy, omit the cloudflared service and configure that proxy instead.

The Staff Applications list searches preferred names, Minecraft usernames, or Discord IDs and filters by Community Staff, Builder, Artist, or Developer and by Received, In review, Approved, or Denied. Enter a name and press Search or Enter to find matching applications across all pages. Name searches ignore capitalization and surrounding spaces. Filters combine and remain in the page URL when opening an application and returning to the list. Reset filters clears the search and both selections. Denied applications remain stored and available to authorized reviewers.

By default, Managers and Founders can open **Edit application questions** from Staff Applications. Each of the four forms has its own description, questions, order, required answers, community conditions, and scenario prompts. Questions support paragraphs, short answers, and single choice answers. Save each form before switching teams. Identity fields, the 18+ requirement, weekly hours, consent, and the required scenario response remain protected. Saved changes apply to new drafts; existing drafts and submitted applications retain their original questions and answers.

## Roles and dashboard permissions

Managers and Founders can open **Roles**. The page lists Founder, Manager, Admin, Sr Mod, Mod, Jr Mod, Helper, Builder, Dev and Artist, with grouped switches for every dashboard action. Changes across roles are saved together. Existing access rules are the defaults; the Roles page can delegate individual features, such as letting Sr Mod read Email without sending mail or changing its read status.

Members receive the combined grants of their roles. A disabled switch does not override another role that grants that permission. Dependent permissions require their parent access. The Dashboard role is required for admission; rank assignments alone do not grant admission. Workspace accounts are created automatically, and the saved Open Tracker and Calendar permission controls workspace access. Role management remains limited to Managers and Founders, even when other feature permissions are delegated. Managers cannot edit Founder permissions. Founder dashboard and role-management access is protected against accidental removal.

The Discord members view searches names or user IDs and lets authorized staff choose one community rank, multiple specialist roles, and the separate staff-server access roles. Only a Founder can assign or remove Manager and Founder ranks, or change those members' access roles. A Founder cannot remove their own Founder rank or Dashboard access. Confirmed changes preserve unrelated Discord roles. Community ranks synchronize between both servers; assignments waiting for a member to join, membership screening or the bot's role hierarchy remain stored and retry. Specialist and access roles currently apply in the staff server only. Minecraft role synchronization is not connected.

Permission revisions and role-change requests are recorded in encrypted storage. Concurrent permission edits and stale Discord member snapshots are rejected rather than overwriting newer changes. The server uses saved permissions immediately; refresh open dashboard pages to update their navigation and controls.

## Public website

The public website has no accounts or forums. Discord remains the community hub.

Public pages use rounded gray panels and solid buttons without decorative borders. A lightly blurred, darkened medieval backdrop fades gently into the page. Navigation, pixel branding and status links stay sharp and readable above the scenery. Keyboard focus uses an underline instead of an outline. At widths of 900 pixels or less, the header uses a hamburger menu with full width navigation links. The menu closes after navigation, when focus leaves it, when clicking outside it, or when returning to the desktop layout. Escape closes it and returns focus to the toggle. Phone layouts use compact panel spacing, stacked server and support buttons, and a two column footer.

The desktop scenery uses a local WebGL smoke density field advected through a curling flow. Mouse motion acts as wind that pushes and swirls existing smoke without sending or storing coordinates. On Home only, a large dragon flies left to right above the content and breathes a downward curtain of fire at a constant angle relative to its mouth and banking motion. It lights the small campfires and castle torches in the pixel artwork, plus two torches beside the gap between the Home welcome and modpack banners. Scenery flames scroll on a document layer with their props and are clipped to each scene or torch; headings remain plain and upright. The fly-in starts after Home content loads. Other public pages keep their ambient scenery without a dragon or breath overlay. Returning to a browser tab resumes the existing animation instead of restarting the fly-in. It can be paused, respects reduced motion, suspends while the tab is hidden, and releases frame callbacks, event listeners and GPU resources on unmount. Screens at or below 900px show the original static scenery without dragon, fire, smoke or added props, and allocate no animation GPU resources. Rendering is capped at 1440 × 900, with a 480 × 320 smoke field and 30 updates per second. Canvas sizes follow CSS layout dimensions so flame centers remain aligned when a scrollbar is present. Unsupported WebGL falls back to a still warm background. The fire and smoke code does not load remote scripts.

### Privacy, Terms and inactivity deletion

`/privacy`, `/terms` and `/privacy-settings` are separate documents on the public and staff origins. Their links also appear beside submission forms. The operator's legal name is supplied through protected deployment configuration, not the source templates. In the private `privacy` configuration object, set `retentionEnabled` and `published` to explicit booleans and `controllerName` to the actual legal operator name. Publication requires enabled retention and a name. Accepting the Terms is separate from consent to optional processing.

With retention enabled, a daily worker removes portal tickets, partnership requests and staff applications after twelve calendar months without owner activity. Owner submissions, replies, preference changes, reopening and ratings renew the relevant record. Verified Discord identity activity and intentional authenticated staff use renew linked portal records; background polling, staff review and scheduled delivery do not count as the applicant's activity. Legacy staff accounts without a known activity timestamp start their timer on activation. The worker removes local child records and waits for in-flight delivery before cleanup. It also removes tracked bot-managed Discord channels, attachments and notices. External cleanup failure keeps a restricted deletion intent for retry. Each sweep attempts at most fifty cases and rotates the starting position. Inactive staff profiles, credentials, Minecraft links, preferences and portal sessions are deleted. Existing shorter expiries remain. Role audit entries and mailbox reply markers expire after twelve months from their event.

Private application update channels are removed only after the owner's last application is erased and the saved channel still has its expected ownership marker. Huly content, Proton mailbox messages, Minecraft server logs and player files, independent store records and manual backups are separate systems; this worker does not delete them or observe gameplay there. Operators must coordinate privacy requests and backup expiry/restores across those systems. A restricted `privacy-hold` record requires a specific reason, scope and review date, and must be reviewed by the operator. It is not a general exemption from deletion.

Any change to collected information, purpose, recipient, retention, contact preference or automated decision must update the Privacy Policy and the relevant collection notice with the implementation. Update the effective date/version for material changes and obtain any separately required consent. The policy is a disclosure, not a certification of network-wide GDPR compliance; provider agreements, transfer safeguards, child privacy and backup procedures also require operational review.
